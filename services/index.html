Plumbing Services in West Chester, PA.

Drains, sewer and septic

Slow drains, backups and failing lines, from a single clogged sink to a damaged lateral under the yard.

Drain Cleaning & Unclogging.

Clears sink, tub, toilet and main line blockages, day or night, after finding out whether it is one fixture or the whole line.

Hydro Jetting.

Washes grease, scale and roots off pipe walls for drains that keep clogging after snaking.

Sewer Line Repair.

Camera-guided repair of cracked, offset or root-damaged sewer laterals, with options from spot repair to replacement.

Sewer Camera Inspection.

A recorded look inside the sewer line, for repeat clogs or before buying a house.

Trenchless Pipe Lining.

Repairs a sound but damaged pipe from small access points, which suits lines under driveways and landscaping.

Sewer Backup Cleanup & Repair.

Emergency response when sewage comes up through a drain, with the cause identified after the line is open.

Septic Tank Pumping.

Pumping and checks for properties on on-lot septic, with the Chester County permit rules explained.

Water heaters and heating

Hot water and heat problems, with repair and replacement options compared honestly.

Water Heater Repair.

Diagnoses no hot water, leaks and noisy tanks, and tells you honestly whether to repair or replace.

Water Heater Installation & Replacement.

Tank, tankless and heat pump options compared, with permit, venting and old unit removal covered.

Tankless Water Heater Service.

Descaling, error codes and flow problems on tankless units.

Boiler Repair.

No-heat and low-pressure faults on hot water and steam boilers, with safety checks first.

Leaks, pipes and water bills

Finding where water is going, and fixing the pipe that is letting it out.

Leak Detection.

Finds hidden leaks in walls, floors, ceilings and service lines using meter tests, acoustic tools and thermal imaging.

Slab Leak Detection & Repair.

Pinpoints and repairs leaks under concrete floors, or reroutes the line overhead.

Burst Pipe Repair.

Emergency repair of split and burst pipes, with the shutoff steps to follow while help is on the way.

Frozen Pipe Repair.

Safe thawing and repair of frozen lines before they split, plus prevention for next winter.

Ceiling & Wall Leak Repair.

Works out whether a stain is a pipe, a roof or condensation, then repairs the plumbing source.

Water Service Line Leak Repair.

Locates and repairs leaks on the buried line between the street and the house.

High Water Bill & Meter Leak.

A meter test and a methodical search for where unexplained water is going.

Repiping & Whole-Home Pipe Replacement.

Replaces failing galvanized, polybutylene or pinhole-prone supply piping through the house.

General Plumbing Repair.

Water hammer, low pressure, failed valves and weeping fittings that do not fit another category.

Basements and water damage

Keeping water out of the basement, and drying out what it reached.

Sump Pump Installation & Repair.

Emergency and scheduled sump pump repair, new pits and battery backups.

Basement Waterproofing.

Works out where basement water comes from and what drainage, grading or pump fix suits it.

Water Damage Restoration.

Drying, cleanup and restoration after a leak or flood, on a separate 24/7 water damage line.

Water Mitigation.

The first 48 hours of a water loss: extraction, drying setup and saving what can be saved.

Fixtures and installations

Everyday repairs and new work at sinks, toilets, disposals and bathrooms.

Faucet & Sink Repair.

Dripping faucets, failed shutoffs and leaking supply lines and traps under the sink.

Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation.

Jams, leaks and dead units, plus new disposal installation and dishwasher hookups.

Toilet Repair & Installation.

Running, leaking and clogged toilets, wax rings and flanges, and new toilet installation.

Plumbing Installation.

New fixtures, relocations and bathroom additions, planned around permits and rough-in.

Water quality and commercial

Treatment, protection and service for larger buildings.

Water Filtration Installation.

Whole-house and under-sink filters and reverse osmosis, matched to what you want reduced.

Water Softener Installation & Repair.

Hardness testing, softener installation and repair of salt, resin and valve faults.

Backflow Testing & Prevention.

Testing, repair and installation of backflow preventers on irrigation, boiler and commercial lines.

Commercial Plumbing.

Restaurants, offices, rental buildings and campus properties, scheduled around business hours.
