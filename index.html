Emergency plumbing

Plumbing emergencies in West Chester, day or night

An emergency plumber in West Chester is a plumber who takes calls at any hour for problems that cannot wait until morning, including burst pipes, sewage backups and water heaters leaking from the tank. This line covers the borough and the townships around it in Chester County, on weekends and holidays as well as weeknights.

Most emergencies give some warning first. A drain that gurgles, a toilet that rises instead of draining or a stain spreading across a ceiling all show up before the flood does. Calling at the first sign usually costs less than calling after the basement is wet, and a clogged drain cleared early or a water heater caught before it fails is often a single visit.

If you cannot tell if your situation counts, the two lists below sort what to handle tonight from what can wait. When water is moving and you cannot stop it, call the 24/7 line.

Emergency or scheduled?

What to handle tonight and what can wait

Call the emergency line now

  • Water is running and you cannot stop it.
  • Sewage is backing up into tubs, showers or floor drains.
  • No water at all, while neighbors still have it.
  • The water heater tank is leaking, or the water is scalding hot.
  • Water is reaching outlets, appliances or the electrical panel.
  • You smell gas: leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility first.

Can wait for a scheduled visit

  • A slow drain at one fixture.
  • A faucet that drips.
  • A toilet that keeps running after a flush.
  • Pipes that knock when a tap shuts off.
  • Low pressure at a single faucet.
  • A water heater that heats slowly but still works.
Before the plumber arrives

Four steps that limit the damage

1

Shut off the main water

The main valve is usually where the water line enters the house, often in a basement or utility area. A wheel handle closes clockwise. A lever closes when it sits crosswise to the pipe.

2

Drain the lines and stay clear of power

Open a cold faucet on the lowest floor to drain the pipes. If water is near outlets or the panel, keep away and switch off power only if the breaker is reachable without standing in water.

3

Isolate a leaking water heater

Switch off the tank at its breaker, or turn the gas control to off. Then close the cold water supply valve on top of the tank so it stops refilling.

4

Document before cleanup

Photograph the damage and the failed part before anything is moved. Your insurer will ask for them, and the plumber can use them to diagnose the cause.

Emergency dispatch

What to tell the emergency plumber dispatcher

The first minute of the call decides how fast the right plumber and the right equipment arrive. Say where the water, sewage or smell is, whether it is still getting worse, and whether you have found the main water valve.

Mention the address and the nearest township, such as West Goshen or East Bradford, and say if the building is a house, a rental or a business. Tell the dispatcher about any recent work on the system, and whether power or gas appliances are near the water.

If you are unsure whether it counts as an emergency, call and describe it. A dispatcher can say whether to shut something off now, or whether a leak detection or drain cleaning visit can be booked.

Local plumbing

What is different about plumbing in West Chester

West Chester became a borough in 1799 and, according to the Documentary History of American Water-works, has run a public water system since July 1841. Aqua Pennsylvania has supplied the water since 1998.

In the older streets, a sudden pressure drop or a wet patch near the curb can point to the service line between the main and your meter, so mention both when you call. Which side of the meter a leak is on usually decides who repairs it.

The borough's plumbing chapter follows the International Plumbing Code with local amendments. It requires a permit for plumbing work and a license for the plumber doing it. Permits go to a master plumber, though an owner may pull one for work done personally on their own single-family home. Ask who is pulling the permit before any water heater replacement or pipe reroute, and ask to see the plumber's license.

Searching for a plumber can turn up listings for Westchester County, New York. That is a different place with its own county plumbing license. Check that the address is in Chester County, Pennsylvania before you book anyone.

Pipes in unheated garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls freeze first when overnight temperatures drop into the teens. If a faucet slows to a trickle on a freezing morning, shut off the main water valve and call before the pipe splits.

Neighborhoods and landmarks

West Chester neighborhoods and landmarks we dispatch to

Plumbing problems follow the age of the buildings, and West Chester has a lot of old ones. These are the places local callers name most often, with what the building stock usually means for the pipes inside it.

Downtown Historic District

Gay and High Streets

The National Register district around Gay and High Streets was listed in 1985 and counts 3,137 contributing buildings built between 1789 and the 1930s. Pipes in buildings that old are often a mix of cast iron, galvanized steel and later copper. Restaurants and shops add grease lines and floor drains to that mix.

Chester County Courthouse area

High and Market Streets

The courthouse was built in 1846 and designed by Thomas U. Walter. Offices and shops around it date from several eras, so one building can hold three generations of plumbing. A camera inspection before a repair shows what is actually in the wall.

Marshall Square

Northeast side of the borough

Marshall Square Park was dedicated in 1848 as the borough's first public square and sits on land bought in 1841 for a reservoir. The houses around it are among the borough's older homes. Cast iron drains and old supply lines are the usual suspects there.

Everhart Park

West edge of the borough

Dr. Isaiah Everhart gave the 10.2-acre grove to the borough in 1905, and Blackhorse Run begins inside it. Mature trees near older homes make root growth in sewer laterals a common cause of repeat clogs. A slow main drain near a big tree is worth a camera look.

West Chester University

North and south campuses

The school opened in 1871 as the West Chester Normal School, and its Quadrangle Historic District was listed in 1981. Student rentals around campus put heavy seasonal use on drains and water heaters. Landlords and tenants call about clogs and no-hot-water problems most when a new term begins.

Gay Street and the east side

Pippin home to Fugett Park

A state marker at 327 Gay Street identifies the home of folk artist Horace Pippin, who was born in West Chester in 1888. Brick rowhouses along Gay Street share walls, which makes a leak in one house a problem for its neighbor. Tell the dispatcher if the wet wall is shared.

Chester County Hospital, Henderson High School and several of the borough's twelve parks sit on the line between the borough and West Goshen. A property near that line may belong to either municipality, so give dispatch your street and the nearest main road. The areas page lists the township pages.
