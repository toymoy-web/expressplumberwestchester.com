Start with what you notice.

My water bill went up and I cannot find a leak.

A water bill that rises with no change in habits, a meter dial that moves with every fixture off, or a stain that appears in a ceiling are the three most common reasons people call for leak detection. In each case the leak is real, and in each case it is somewhere you cannot see.

The first job is to figure out whether the leak is inside the house or between the meter and the house, because the repair and the responsibility differ.

The meter test you can do yourself.

This takes about fifteen minutes and tells you whether to call.

1

Turn everything off.

Close taps, stop the washing machine and dishwasher, and make sure the ice maker and any humidifier are not running.

2

Read the meter.

Note the position of the small leak indicator or the last digits on the dial. Take a photo.

3

Wait fifteen minutes.

Do not use any water during the wait, including flushing a toilet.

4

Compare.

If the indicator or digits moved, water is passing the meter with nothing on, which means a leak.

Methods plumbers use, and what each finds.

Method.Finds.Limit.
Pressure isolation.Which supply line is losing pressure.Does not show the exact spot.
Acoustic listening.The sound of water escaping from pressurized pipe.Quiet leaks and noisy rooms are harder.
Thermal imaging.Temperature differences from hot water leaks or wet insulation.Needs a temperature contrast.
Moisture meter.Wet drywall, subfloor or framing.Shows damage, not always the source.
Camera inspection.Interior condition of drain lines.Not for pressurized supply leaks.

Which side of the meter is the leak on?

Aqua Pennsylvania supplies the borough system, and its 2025 report notes that a service line inventory is available at aquawater.com/leadmap. Aqua's customer line is 877-987-2782. If a leak is on the utility side of the meter, the utility handles it. If it is on your side, the repair is yours, and the plumber needs to know the answer before digging.

That is why detection starts with the meter and then moves inside: pressure isolation tells you whether the loss is on the line from the meter or in branches inside the house.

After the leak is found.

The next step depends on where it is. A leak under the floor leads to locating a leak under the floor, one between the meter and the house to fixing the buried pipe from the street, and a wet ceiling to ceiling leak repair.

What to do while you wait for detection.

Close the main if water is spreading and move valuables off wet floors. Detection methods are chosen according to the pipe material and the access available, so tell the plumber what is behind the suspect wall.
