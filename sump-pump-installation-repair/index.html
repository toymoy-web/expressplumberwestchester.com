Why it fails.

The four ways a sump pump lets you down.

A sump pump fails in a handful of predictable ways. The float switch jams against the side of the pit, the motor burns out after years of cycling, the check valve sticks and lets water run back into the pit, or the power goes out during the same storm that fills the pit.

Each has a different fix, and the last is the reason a battery backup is worth considering. A pump that has run constantly for days is also a warning that groundwater or a nearby leak is feeding the pit.

Emergency service.

Emergency sump pump repair in West Chester: when to call tonight.

A pump that has stopped during a storm, a pit that is filling, or a basement taking on water is an emergency, because the water table does not wait. Calls come in during heavy rain, so start now.

Check whether the pump has power and whether the float moves freely. If the pit is filling and the pump is silent, say so to the dispatcher.

What Ida taught Chester County basements.

In September 2021 the remnants of Hurricane Ida dropped about 6 to 8 inches of rain on Chester County in a matter of hours, and Brandywine Creek crested at record levels. Chester County Emergency Services reported more than 4,000 calls and over 300 rescues within ten hours, and West Chester Borough saw downed trees and flooded roads.

For a homeowner, the practical lesson is simple. A pump that works in an ordinary storm may not keep up in an extreme one, and the power may fail at the same time. A second pump or a battery backup is what protects a finished basement.

Quick checks if the pump is not running.

1

Check the power.

Confirm the pump is plugged into a working outlet, the GFCI has not tripped, and the breaker is on.

2

Look at the float.

Lift the float switch by hand. If the pump starts, the float was stuck or tangled against the pit wall.

3

Listen for the motor.

A hum without pumping may mean a jammed impeller. Keep your hands out of the pit while the pump is plugged in.

4

Check the discharge.

Make sure the discharge pipe outside is open and not frozen or buried in debris, and that the check valve is moving.
