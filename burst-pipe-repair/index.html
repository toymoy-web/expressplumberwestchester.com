First five minutes.

What to do right now.

1

Shut off the main.

The main is usually where the water line enters the house. A wheel handle closes clockwise. A lever closes when crosswise to the pipe.

2

Drain the pipes.

Open a cold faucet on the lowest floor so water already in the lines drains.

3

Mind the electrics.

If water is near outlets or the panel, keep away and switch off power only if the breaker is dry and reachable.

4

Photograph, then call.

Take pictures of the damage and the pipe for your insurer, then call the 24/7 line.

Emergency service.

Emergency burst pipe repair in West Chester: when to call tonight.

A pipe that has split, a supply line spraying water, or a ceiling dripping after a freeze is an emergency every time. Burst pipe repair starts with the shutoff and ends with a tested, dry line.

Call as soon as water is moving. Tell the dispatcher whether you have found the main shutoff and where the water is showing up.

What to do until the emergency plumber arrives.

1

Close the main water valve.

Turn it clockwise until water stops. Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the line.

2

Turn off the water heater.

Switch off power or gas so the tank does not run dry.

3

Mop and photograph.

Remove standing water, move belongings and take photos for insurance.

Finding your main shutoff before you need it.

Most houses have the main shutoff where the water line enters the building, often on a basement wall near the front, in a utility room or in a crawlspace. In houses with a meter inside, there may be two valves, one before the meter and one after. The one after the meter is yours to use.

Test it once a year by closing and reopening it. A valve that has not moved in years can seize, and discovering that during a burst is the worst time. If it will not close fully, tell the plumber, because replacing it is a small job that makes every later emergency easier.

When the temperature falls.

Frozen water expands about nine percent, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls split first once overnight lows reach the teens. Frozen water expands, and the split usually appears at the weakest point after the ice melts, not while it is frozen.

If a faucet slows to a trickle on a freezing morning, shut off the main and call before the ice thaws. If you must thaw a pipe, use a hair dryer on low heat. Never use an open flame. The prevention page, frozen pipe repair, covers more.

After the pipe is fixed.

Drying the structure is a separate job from fixing the pipe. For that, see drying and cleanup after a leak and the first 48 hours of a water loss, which use a separate phone line. If a leak is not visible, leak detection visits helps find it.

Pipe materials and how they fail.

If several sections fail in a short time, the pipe is aging as a system. That is a signal to price a repipe rather than patching one failure after another.

What to do in the first minutes.

Close the main, drain the lines from a low tap and keep clear of wet outlets. Pipe repairs follow the plumbing code and the pipe maker's standards for fittings and pressure ratings.

If a pipe lets go in Westtown, the page for that township lists the emergency steps in the order to take them. Close the main first, then call.

A split pipe in a downtown building with several tenants can affect the units below, so closing the building's main is the first step and calling is the second. Photograph the damage before anything is moved.

Cracked, broken and copper pipe repair.

A cracked elbow, a split copper line or a failed PVC connector can be repaired with a section replacement. CPVC and older materials may need different fittings.

If the pipe fails in several places, the plumber may recommend replacing the section or the whole run instead of repeating repairs.
