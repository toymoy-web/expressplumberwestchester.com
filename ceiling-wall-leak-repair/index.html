Is the stain from plumbing or the roof?

Not every ceiling stain comes from a pipe. Roof leaks, window leaks, condensation on cold ducts and plumbing leaks can all leave a brown ring. The pattern helps. A stain that grows after rain points to the roof.

A stain under a bathroom or kitchen points to plumbing. A stain that appears when the washing machine or shower runs points to a drain or supply line above.

A plumber starts by testing which fixtures above the stain are used when the stain changes.

What to do in the first hour with a wet ceiling.

Move belongings out from under the stain and place a bucket below any drip. If the ceiling is bulging, a small hole at the lowest point of the bulge lets the water drain in a controlled way instead of dumping across the room when the drywall fails.

Then try to identify what is above. If a bathroom is overhead, stop using it. If a washing machine or dishwasher is overhead, stop it. If the stain grows without any water running, shut off the main and call, because a supply pipe is leaking under pressure.

Mold and the 48-hour window.

Wet drywall and insulation can start growing mold within a couple of days. Dry the area, open it up if it stays wet, and do not paint over a stain until the source is fixed and the area is dry. If the area is large, see drying and cleanup after a leak, which uses a separate phone number.

Related work.

If the leak is hidden and not obvious, start with a hidden-leak search. For a pipe that has split, see the steps for a burst pipe. A high bill may come with it: meter leak testing.

Repairs that follow the plumbing fix.

  • Dry the area fully, using fans and dehumidifiers if the cavity is wet.
  • Remove any drywall or insulation that stayed saturated, because it will grow mold if left.
  • Treat or seal the stain with a stain-blocking primer once the surface is dry.
  • Patch and paint the access hole, which the plumber leaves neat to make this easy.
  • Keep photographs and the plumber's invoice together in case the insurer asks.

Skipping the drying step to repaint quickly is the most common mistake. A fresh coat over damp drywall can bubble, peel and hide a mold problem.

What to do before you cut into drywall.

Switch off power to a room with a dripping ceiling fixture and drain a bulge through a small hole into a bucket. Wet drywall can hold mold, so follow EPA moisture and mold guidelines for drying and replacement.

In West Goshen a ceiling stain can be in a house or in a multi-tenant building, and the second case adds the question of who owns the pipe above. Tell the dispatcher which it is.

In multi-story brick buildings downtown, a stain on a ceiling often comes from the unit above. Photograph the stain, note the time, and tell the dispatcher which floor the water is coming from.
