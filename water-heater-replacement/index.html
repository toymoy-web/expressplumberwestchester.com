Decide the type.

Three ways to replace a water heater.

A like-for-like tank swap is the quickest and the cheapest to install. A tankless unit takes less space and delivers hot water on demand, but it needs gas line sizing and venting that a tank does not. A heat pump water heater uses electricity to move heat instead of making it, which uses much less power but needs room and airflow around it.

If the old heater has already failed, the fastest option is usually a matching tank. If you have time to plan, the other two are worth a quote.

What a proper replacement includes.

These are the steps that separate a clean installation from a repeat call.

  • Draining and disconnecting the old unit, and carrying it out.
  • Checking the gas line, vent and combustion air on gas units, or the circuit and breaker size on electric ones.
  • Installing a new temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe run to a safe spot.
  • Fitting new flexible connectors and shutoff valves on the water lines.
  • Adding an expansion tank where the plumbing is a closed system, which is common once a pressure-reducing valve is present.
  • Filling, purging air, lighting or powering up, and confirming the temperature and no leaks.

Permits and the borough code.

In West Chester Borough, replacing a water heater is plumbing work under the borough's plumbing code, which issues permits to a master plumber. Owners may pull a permit for work they do personally on their own single-family home, but gas connections and venting make that a poor do-it-yourself project.

Ask the plumber who is pulling the permit and whether an inspection is required. The surrounding townships each run their own permit office, so the answer for East Goshen or Westtown can differ from the borough's.

Choosing with West Chester homes in mind.

Aqua Pennsylvania supplies the West Chester system and its 2025 report names Brandywine Creek and two wells as the sources. The report does not list hardness, so if you see white scale on fixtures, a water test can show whether a softener is worth adding to protect the new unit.

If the existing tank only needs a part, repairing a water heater may be cheaper than replacement. Tankless owners with error codes should look at tankless service before replacing anything.

What to do if the old tank is leaking now.

Switch off power or gas, close the cold supply and keep clear of hot discharge. Installations follow the plumbing code, including relief valve and venting standards, and the manufacturer's guidelines.

A tank that has started to leak in a garage or basement is a reason to call at any hour in East Goshen. The township page lists the roads dispatch covers.

Houses around Marshall Square, the borough's first public square dedicated in 1848, were built long before today's heater sizes and vent rules. Measure the basement stair and the existing flue before the visit, because older homes can limit which tank fits.
