Neighborhood and geography first.

Emergency plumbing in a township built along two branches of the Brandywine.

East Bradford was organized around 1705 as part of Bradford Township and became its own township when Bradford was split in 1731. The East Branch of Brandywine Creek reaches the main Brandywine within the township, and Creek Road follows the East Branch along its northern edge. Streams and valleys shape the lots, and they shape the water problems.

Homes near the creek valleys deal with groundwater and storm runoff more often than homes on higher ground. A sump pump that runs a few times in a normal storm may run constantly in a wet week, and that is the first piece of plumbing to check when the weather turns.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

What to do to limit damage in East Bradford while a plumber is on the way.

  • Close the main water valve where the line enters the house, then open a cold tap on the lowest floor to drain the pipes.
  • If the leak is near the creek side of the house, check the sump pit before anything else and keep away from the panel if the basement floor is wet.
  • A leaking water heater needs its breaker or gas control off and its cold supply closed.
  • Photograph the split pipe and the water line on the wall before you wipe anything up.
  • Call the 24/7 line and give the dispatcher East Bradford and the nearest cross street, such as Creek Road or Copeland School Road.

Creek flooding and the lesson of 2021.

During the remnants of Hurricane Ida on 1 and 2 September 2021, Brandywine Creek flooded at record levels and Chester County Emergency Services reported more than 4,000 calls within ten hours. One West Chester homeowner living along the Brandywine described having only moments to leave when water rose.

For East Bradford homeowners the practical response is to keep the sump pump serviced, add a battery backup, and know where the main shutoff is. If water enters the house, basement waterproofing looks at long-term drainage. The neighboring township to the west, West Bradford, faces the same creek system.
