If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

What to do to limit damage in West Goshen while a plumber is on the way.

  • Close the main, and in a shop or office also close the branch valve for the affected restroom or kitchen.
  • Move stock, paper and electronics off the floor in rooms where water is spreading.
  • Keep staff and customers away from wet floors and from outlets near the leak.
  • Photograph the leak, the shutoff you used and the damage, and note the time you closed the valve.
  • Call the 24/7 line, say West Goshen, and name the nearest main road such as Paoli Pike or Route 3.
