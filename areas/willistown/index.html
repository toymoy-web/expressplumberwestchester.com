Roads that are older than the houses.

Three cowpaths, a crossroads village and a township of creeks.

Willistown was organized as a township in 1704, and in 1710 three cowpaths were established here called Goshen, Sugartown and Boot. All three roads still carry those names. Sugartown, White Horse and Plumsock were the earliest villages.

The township covers about 18 square miles, and the Willistown Conservation Trust describes its work as reaching across the Crum Creek, Ridley Creek and Darby Creek watersheds. A house on low ground near any stream is the one most likely to meet groundwater in a long wet spell. That makes the sump pit the first thing worth checking when a storm settles in.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

What to do to limit damage in Willistown while a plumber is on the way.

  • Close the main water valve, then open the lowest cold tap to relieve the pressure in the pipes.
  • On a septic property, stop running water after a backup and do not flush to test, because more water pushes into a tank that is already full.
  • If a private well feeds the house, switch off the pump at its breaker so it stops pushing water into the leak.
  • Photograph the split fitting and the wet wall or floor before anything is wiped up, and note the time you closed the valve.
  • Call the 24/7 line, say Willistown, and name the nearest road such as Sugartown Road, Goshen Road, Boot Road or Street Road.

Township sewer, Aqua Wastewater and the septic tank in the yard.

A January 2024 township handout lists about 2,334 sewer customers across four districts and says the township maintains ownership of the system. It adds that most properties outside those districts are served either by on-site septic or by Aqua Wastewater. A 2023 news report put the number of households on septic at about 2,000.

That mix is why the first question on any drain call is which system you have. If a septic tank is full, a backup is a pumping job and pumping a septic tank comes first. If the line runs to a public sewer, a blockage in the lateral is handled through what to do when sewage comes up, and the plumber can say who is responsible for which section.

Septic permits in Chester County come from the Chester County Health Department at 601 Westtown Road, Suite 295, West Chester. Sewer connection questions for township districts go to the township office on Sugartown Road.
