If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

What to do to limit damage in West Bradford while a plumber is on the way.

  • Shut the main valve, which in an older stone house may be in a cellar corner or a crawlspace, and open the lowest tap.
  • Do not guess in a house with mixed old and new pipe: if a valve will not close, leave it and say so on the phone.
  • If you are on a private well, switch off the pump at its breaker so it does not keep feeding the leak.
  • Note which wall or floor is wet and take photographs for your insurer.
  • Call the 24/7 line, say West Bradford, and name the nearest road such as Strasburg Road or Downingtown Pike.
Village and history first.

Older houses and what they mean for an emergency pipe call.

West Bradford was organized in 1705 and split into East and West Bradford in 1731. Marshallton, a village on a ridge between West Chester and Downingtown, was listed as a National Register Historic District in 1988, covering 67 buildings. The township has three historic districts in all: Marshallton, Trimblesville and Northbrook.

A house with 18th- or 19th-century roots usually has a patchwork of plumbing from several eras. A newer kitchen may connect to an older bathroom line, and the old section is where leaks hide. When a plumber works on a house like that, they should test pressure through the whole run and tell you what they find before quoting.

Private wells and septic in the township.

Some West Bradford properties are on public water and sewer and some are not. If your home uses a private well or an on-lot septic system, the Chester County Health Department, not the township, issues permits and enforces the rules. Its permit line is 610-344-6225.

That changes who you call for what. A failing septic tank is a septic pumping call. A pressure tank that cycles constantly may need attention before the pump burns out. And a leak inside the house is a standard leak detection job regardless of the water source.

Both branches of the Brandywine.

The township lies between the East and West Branches of Brandywine Creek, which means water finds its way to low ground quickly in a long storm. When the remnants of Ida passed on 1 and 2 September 2021, the East Branch near Downingtown reached major flood levels. A sump pump with a battery backup is the best insurance for a basement here. The neighboring township to the east, East Bradford, shares the creek system.
