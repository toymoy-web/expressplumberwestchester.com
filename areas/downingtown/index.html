Where the borough sits.

A mill borough in the low ground beside the East Branch.

Settlers arrived here in 1716, and the place was first called Milltown because of the grain and paper mills strung along the East Branch of the Brandywine. It took its present name from Thomas Downing, a Quaker immigrant who owned mills in the area. The borough covers about 2.2 square miles and had 7,892 residents at the 2020 census.

The Downingtown Log House, dated to about 1700, and the stone General Washington Inn still stand along the old route that is now Lancaster Avenue and U.S. Route 30. Buildings from that long history sit next to newer ones, so the pipes behind one wall may have little in common with the pipes behind the next. A plumber arriving on a call here should expect to find mixed materials and unmarked shutoffs.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

What to do to limit damage in Downingtown while a plumber is on the way.

  • Close the main water valve, which in an older borough house may sit behind a basement stair, in a crawlspace corner or beside the meter.
  • Open a tap on the lowest floor to drain the lines, and leave the hot tap closed if the leak is at the water heater.
  • If water is coming up through a floor drain, stop using every sink, tub and toilet, because the same line feeds them all.
  • Photograph the wet wall, the failed fitting and any water line on the floor before cleanup starts.
  • Call the 24/7 line and say Downingtown, plus the nearest main street such as Lancaster Avenue or Wallace Avenue.

What the creek has done here, and what it means for your pipes.

The East Branch of the Brandywine drops from high ground to about 239 feet above sea level at Downingtown, and Parke Run and Beaver Creek join in the same low stretch. Borough officials have described that position as a hard one, because water from a large upstream area arrives here at once.

In early September 2021 the remnants of Hurricane Ida pushed the creek into the business district and nearby neighborhoods, and residents said they had never seen it so high.

The borough later opened a voluntary buyout program aimed at homeowners with repeat flooding, which tells you how often some properties take water. For a house in the low ground, a serviced sump pump with a battery backup is the cheapest protection, and basement waterproofing looks at drainage for the long term.

Wastewater treatment here is run by the Downingtown Area Regional Authority. The borough formed it in 1985 with East Caln, Uwchlan, West Whiteland and Caln, so a sewage backup that hits a whole street is worth reporting to the borough too.
